About Narendra Modi

Narendra Damodardas Modi is an Indian politician who has served as the Prime Minister of India since May 2014. He is a senior leader of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP).

Before becoming Prime Minister, he served as the Chief Minister of Gujarat from 2001 to 2014.

Early Life

Narendra Damodardas Modi was born on 17 September 1950 in Vadnagar, Gujarat, India.

He grew up in a modest family and developed an interest in public service from an early age.

During his early years, he became associated with the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), which influenced his political development.

Education

  • School education in Vadnagar, Gujarat
  • Bachelor's degree from the University of Delhi
  • Master's degree in Political Science from Gujarat University

Political Career

Chief Minister of Gujarat

Narendra Modi became the Chief Minister of Gujarat in October 2001 and served in the position until May 2014.

Prime Minister of India

Narendra Modi became the Prime Minister of India in May 2014 after the Bharatiya Janata Party won the general election.

Second Term

In 2019, the BJP won another majority in the Lok Sabha, and Narendra Modi began his second term as Prime Minister.

Third Term

Following the 2024 general election, Narendra Modi began his third consecutive term as Prime Minister of India.
